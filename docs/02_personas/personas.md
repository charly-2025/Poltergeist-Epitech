
Alex — 24 years old 

Profile 

    Student / young active person 

    Uses social networks a lot 

    Does not like to expose one’s identity online 

Context 

Alex often has thoughts, opinions or feelings that he doesn’t dare to publish on classic platforms. 
He is hindered by: 

    the public profiles 

    the gaze of others 

    the fear that his words remain attached to his identity 

Objectives 

    Express oneself freely and quickly 

    Publish without creating an account 

    Remain completely anonymous 

Motivations 

    Freedom of expression 

    Instantaneity 

    Lack of social judgment 

Behaviours 

    Scroll a lot 

    Position especially when an emotion is strong 

    Prefers short and direct messages 

frustrations 

    Obligation to create an account everywhere 

    Social pressure (likes, followers, image) 

    Fear of leaving permanent traces 

Expectations regarding the application 

    No registration 

    No nickname, no photo 

    A simple interface centered on the text

Persona 3  

Name 

Yanis — 27 years old 

Profile 

    Comfortable with digital tools 

    Curious, sociable but discreet 

    Does not like superficial conversations 

Context 

Yanis sometimes comes across an anonymous message that strongly interpels him 
(emotion, opinion, personal situation). 
He would like to answer, but only if the exchange makes sense. 

Objectives 

    Getting in touch with an interesting person 

    Have a private and sincere discussion 

    Keep anonymity on both sides 

Motivations 

    Human connection 

    Quality rather than quantity 

    Payment as a serious filter 

Behaviours 

    Reads the tweets carefully 

    Click rarely, but voluntarily 

    Agrees to pay if the perceived value is clear 

frustration 

    Useless or toxic discussions 

    Trolls and spam 

    Free interactions without commitment 

Expectations 

    Clearly understand why he is paying 

    A simple and secure discussion 

    No profile, no trace after the exchange
