## Objectives
- Allow users to create threads and comments without creating an account
- Ensure full anonymity by default
- Provide a clear and readable discussion structure
- Reduce friction to posting (less than 10 seconds to publish)
- Enable basic moderation to limit abuse

## Assumptions
- Users are more likely to post if no account is required
- Anonymity increases engagement
- Simple moderation can reduce toxic behavior

## Risks
- Abuse due to anonymity
- Spam or low-quality content
- Legal and moderation challenges

## Constraints
- No user authentication system
