⬆️ **The Terms and Conditions** page is typically the first thing you’ll encounter. The ghost icon comes from the name **Poltergeist**—a term used to describe a spirit that haunts a specific place.

This symbolism reflects the core ideas behind the website: the ghost represents anonymity, while the concept of haunting refers to being tied to whatever city/place you choose to see the post of.



![[localizationpoltergeist.webp]]

⬆️ **Localization page**, where you choose the city whose posts you want to see.

We deliberately kept options and features to a minimum. In a world where every platform tries to do everything. Every social media has timelines, endless scrolling, stories, messages, calls, and group chats, here, **Poltergeist** stays focused on its core purpose.

_“If you try to please everyone, you will please no one.”_  
— **Aesop** (paraphrased)

![[threadspoltergeist.webp]]

⬆️ This is the **main page**, where the arguing happens.

The green-on-black theme nods to terminal interfaces and hacker culture, a natural fit for gamers and geeks. Dark mode comes by default, because if you’re going to spend hours debating with strangers online, you shouldn’t have to sacrifice your eyesight too.



Regarding accessibility, we researched the most common visual and cognitive conditions that can affect how people experience websites. Thanks to its minimalist structure and high-contrast color scheme, the interface is designed to be broadly accessible and easy to navigate for most users.
